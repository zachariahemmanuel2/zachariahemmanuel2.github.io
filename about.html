Biography

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Georgia State University's Andrew Young School of Policy Studies, where I expect to complete my degree in June 2027 under the guidance of Dr. James H. Marton. I am an applied microeconomist with research interests in health economics, public economics, and demographic economics.

My work uses causal inference and applied econometric methods to study health insurance, healthcare access, preventive health policies, disability, and the distributional effects of public policy. A central goal of my research is to understand how policies affect individuals and families, particularly populations that may bear unequal health or economic burdens.
