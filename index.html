I am an applied microeconomist. My research lies at the intersection of health, public, and demographic economics, with a focus on how health policies and interventions affect vulnerable populations.

My work uses causal inference methods to study health insurance, healthcare access, preventive health policies, disability, and the distributional effects of public policy.
