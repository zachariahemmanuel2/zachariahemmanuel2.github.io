Welcome to my website!

I'm an applied microeconomist. My research lies at the intersection of health, public, and demographic economics, with a focus on how health policies and interventions affect vulnerable populations. In general, I study how health and social policies shape the well-being of low-income families, women, children, people with disabilities, and older adults.

I'm currently on the 2026–2027 job market and will be completing my Ph.D. in the Summer of 2027 under the guidance of Professor James H. Marton. You can find my research on my CV and Google Scholar.
