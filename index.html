I am an applied microeconomist. My research lies at the intersection of health, public, and demographic economics, with a focus on how health policies and interventions affect vulnerable populations.

My work uses causal inference and applied econometric methods to study health insurance, healthcare access, preventive health policies, disability, and the distributional effects of public policy. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in June 2027 under the guidance of Dr. James H. Marton.

You can find some of my earlier publications on my CV and Google Scholar. I am also on Linkedin.
